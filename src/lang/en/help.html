AI_Rulet is a roulette of ideas for artificial intelligence projects. Press “Find”, and a task arrives from the depths of space — one that one or two developers can take to a prototype, with a description, a technology stack and a plan for the first steps.

How to use it

  1. Press “Find” or the Space / Enter key. The search takes about three seconds, then the idea settles in the center.
  2. The idea card appears below — read it and see if it fits.
  3. “↻ Find another” gives the next idea, “⧉ Copy” puts the card on the clipboard in Markdown, “✧ Add to constellation” saves the idea.
  4. “⤴ Share” gives a link to this idea: on a phone the system share menu opens, on a computer the link is copied. Whoever opens it sees the same card.
  5. “Analyze with AI: ↗ Google, ↗ ChatGPT, ↗ Claude” opens the chosen service in a new tab (for Google — AI Mode on Gemini) with the idea and a request to assess it: feasibility, alternatives, risks, where to start. This needs the internet; Google AI Mode isn’t available in every country, and Claude asks you to sign in.

What’s on the card

Area
the AI field the idea belongs to, and its figurative name.
Stack
tools that make it easy to build a prototype.
Path to prototype
five steps: three specific to this idea and two closing steps shared by its area.
Anomaly
an extra challenge for those who find the main task too easy. In the catalog it is either the idea’s own or one shared by its area.

Where the ideas come from

Catalog
a curated collection: in . Each idea has its own path, and its anomaly is either its own or one shared by the area.
Mix
an idea assembled on the fly from a field of life, an AI technique, a product format and an anomaly — . Only compatible parts are combined: the technique needs data that the field really has, and the format and anomaly are matched to that data. A Mix card is a paragraph with a format and an anomaly, without a path.

No repeats

In the Catalog, ideas are dealt like cards from a deck: each one once until the round is complete. Then the deck is reshuffled and never starts with the last idea you got. The Mix has no deck, but the field and the technique never repeat the previous draw.

Buttons at the top

?Help
this panel.
Chronicle and constellation
the last 20 ideas you found and the ones you saved. Any of them can be opened again; the constellation can be exported to a .md file.
Space settings
search animation — four to choose from, each with its own sound; source — Catalog or Mix; the areas catalog ideas are drawn from.
♪Sound
off by default.

Esc closes the open panel.

Where your data lives

The app is a single file: it works offline and sends nothing anywhere on its own. The idea text goes to an external service only when you click one of the “Analyze with AI” links; a “Share” link contains only a short idea code. The chronicle, constellation, deck progress and settings are stored only in this browser — shared across all language versions. If you clear site data or open the file in another browser, everything starts from scratch.

The background is a living space: stars, nebulae, planets and constellations named after AI concepts. While you read a card it dims so as not to distract you.
